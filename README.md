One sentence pitching a ridiculous invention that fixes a tiny everyday annoyance, for the make.fun **One-Sentence Absurd Invention Pitch** bounty. The house judge ranks pitches on how funny, clever and original they are.

> **A houseplant that files a formal HR complaint every time you forget to water it, then graciously forgives you in writing once you apologise to the fern in person.**

## Why this pitch?

The annoyance is tiny and universal: forgetting to water a houseplant. The invention is absurd — a plant with access to HR — yet the mechanism is weirdly plausible office behaviour: a formal complaint, followed by a written apology and forgiveness. The laugh comes from the invention itself (a fern doing paperwork), not from a joke bolted on at the end, and the whole thing lands in one clean sentence.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the 162-character pitch (one sentence, UTF-8, no trailing newline) to `/output/entry.txt` and verifies it meets the bounty gate: 1–220 characters.
